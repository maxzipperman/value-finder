# College football weather playbook (v2: Rule B + Rule HT)

Same rule and gates as the NFL playbook v2 (`../nfl-weather/STRATEGY.md`), on its
own evidence and its own forward test.

## Rule B: early wind under (forward test)

| | |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | FBS game at an outdoor venue with known coordinates and kickoff time; kickoff wind forecast **≥ 15 mph** on the station scale (frozen calibration: station = 1.01 + 0.92 × model wind, fit 2016–23), **1–3 days out**. *Amendment 3:* "kickoff wind" is the average over the kickoff hour and the next three hours, as in the historical evidence; days are calendar days. |
| **Price gate** | Posted total, under at **−115 or better**, positive expected value at that line and price (from the 2006–23 cohort of windy outdoor games). *Amendment 3:* the pricing model is registered in full in `PREREGISTRATION.md`. At the rule's own number the value only reaches zero at about −131, so inside the −115 cap this gate can't reject a bet. *Amendment 4 (Sep 29):* the zero point is about −130, and at −115 the model rejects an under from 1.5 points below the reference. The alert also names the best number any logged book offers, with its value. |
| **Stake** | Paper until 20 settled signals show positive average CLV; if betting, flat 0.5% of bankroll. No size-ups. |
| **Evidence for** | 56.6% under vs the close in 990 observed-wind games 2006–25 (+8% at −110); 57.4% in 2016–25 and 56.4% in 2021–25; 60.9% vs the opener (614 games) with totals falling 1.4 points by kickoff. The closing total moves about 0.9 points for wind that costs 3.7 points of scoring (15–19 mph). |
| **Evidence against** | All of it uses observed airport-station weather, not forecasts; no CFB forecast replay exists yet. Station wind is not stadium wind. Rain unders faded to 51% in 2021–25. *Note, Sep 29:* a forecast replay does exist ([`scripts/forecast_replay.py`](scripts/forecast_replay.py), described in the [README](README.md#forecast-replay-202425)). On the forecasts that existed at bet time, 2024–25, the rule went 61–46–3 (57.0%) at the close, one-sided p = 0.19, against 56.6% on observed wind; two seasons can't confirm it. |
| **Decision** | Scored from Oct 1, 2026 by `scripts/score_forward.py` at entry line and price, CLV against the last quote before kickoff (amendment 2 adds a secondary CLV against the close captured 2–20 minutes before kickoff). Keep only if average CLV > 0 with a 95% interval above zero after 40 signals or the end of the regular season, whichever is later. *Amendment 4 (Sep 29), readings 1 to 12:* a bet whose game moved more than 24 hours, or has no result 30 days after kickoff, is void and not graded (1); the decision waits for every bet that kicked off by its horizon (2); the first final decision is written to `data/forward/decisions.csv` and stands, and a lost record is restored from its nightly copy on the ledgers branch, never decided again unless it is lost before that night's copy is made (3); the horizon is the later of Dec 12, 2026 and the 40th settled signal's kickoff (4); the primary close is the last logged quote later than the entry row, else the captured close, else none (the bet is counted and left out of the CLV) (6); "not kept" means no money goes on the rule, and it stays on paper for 2027 only by a dated amendment before 2027 Week 0 (7); a postponed game that signals again is two listings, and the one that matches the actual kickoff is graded (10); "before kickoff" is before the earlier of the row's kickoff and the schedule's (11); a decision in which fewer than 20 signals have a primary close is inconclusive (12). *Amendment 5 (Sep 29):* the 95% interval is grouped by game day: the signals are grouped by the Eastern date of their game's kickoff, the interval uses Student's t on one less than the number of game days, and with fewer than 2 game days there is no interval and the result is inconclusive; the plain interval is printed beside it, for reference. In simulation, with no edge, it keeps the rule about 3.6 to 4.0% of the time (the plain interval 5.2 to 6.3%), against the intended 2.5%. |

## Rule HT: high-total under (forward test, amendment 1)

From [issue #4](https://github.com/maxzipperman/value-finder/issues/4) and strategy-research idea 2. It doesn't use weather.

| | |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | FBS game on the board, kicking off from 2026 Week 6 (first kickoff 2026-10-07 00:00 UTC) through the 2027 national title game. The posted total is **at least the prior season's mean closing total + 10**. |
| **Threshold** | **2026: 62.6** (the 2025 mean of 52.62 over 955 games, + 10). **2027:** the 2026 mean + 10, from the same data and filters, known before 2027 Week 0 (`board.ht_threshold`). The mean uses cfbfastR consensus closing totals for games with a result and a nonzero closing spread, the set `strategy-research/screen.py` used. |
| **Entry** | The game's **last logged quote before kickoff**, which is the latest number available, at that total and under price. The under must be **−115 or better**. The alert fires on the last scheduled run before kickoff (*amendment 3* fixed the code to do exactly that; a quote logged by hand after it becomes the entry). A game can also be a Rule B signal; the two rules are graded separately. *Amendment 4:* a quote is a posted total with a valid under price, so a later row with no usable under price doesn't replace the entry. |
| **Price source** | The Odds API: Pinnacle, else DraftKings. With no key and no ESPN price, a game can't signal. |
| **Stake** | Paper for both seasons. |
| **Evidence for** | **373–273 (57.7%) in 2016–25**, above 50% in 9 of 10 seasons (screen). *Note, Sep 28:* on the corrected spread data (#36, which recovered a third of the spread pairs the parser silently dropped) the pre-check's by-spread split sums to 502–393 (56.1%), or 434–326 (57.1%) on FBS-involved games; the screen's row is unchanged. The rule, threshold and forward test are as registered; the history is simply restated on more games. The calibration slope of result on closing total fell from 1.01 (2006–15) to 0.89 ± 0.03 (2016–25). It holds with windy and dome games excluded (57.5%). About 65 bets a season, 63% of them from Week 6 on. |
| **Evidence against** | p = 0.0035 against a Bonferroni bar of 0.00046 for 109 variants (*note, Sep 29:* on the day amendment 4 is registered the running count is 271 variants, so the bar is p < 0.000185; amendment 4 tests nothing and leaves the count unchanged). The era split was chosen after looking (2006–15 was 48.2%). The mirror rule (low totals → over) fails (52.0%). The evidence uses consensus closes, while the forward test uses one book's last quote. |
| **Grading** | Win rate and ROI at the entry price; pushes return the stake (*amendment 4:* a push is left out of the exact test and counts as a bet in ROI; the scorer reports the bets by price source). CLV isn't the metric, because the rule bets at the close on the claim that the close is wrong. Graded by `scripts/score_forward.py` (RULE_HT). |
| **Decision** | Made once, after the 2027 season, on 2026 Weeks 6+ and 2027 pooled. **Promote** to the 0.5% stake only if the record beats the break-even of the prices taken with a one-sided binomial p < 0.05 and ROI > 0. **Drop** if the win rate is at or below that break-even. **Otherwise** keep it on paper for 2028 under the same rule. There are no interim decisions. *Note, Sep 29:* amendment 3 (section 4) reads the drop test at the prices taken: drop when ROI at the prices taken is zero or below. Amendment 4 adds that the first final decision is written to `data/forward/decisions.csv` and stands. *Amendment 4, readings 3, 5, 10 and 11 (Sep 29):* the record is restored from its nightly copy if lost, never decided again unless it is lost before that night's copy is made; the entry is the last quote with a valid under price; a postponed game is graded on the listing that matches its actual kickoff; before kickoff is before the earlier of the row's and the schedule's kickoff. The 20-close limit (reading 12) doesn't apply to Rule HT. |
| **Power** | About 105 bets are expected. At −110 that needs about 62% to pass. If the true rate is the screen's 57.7%, the chance of passing is only about 22–27%, so the most likely outcome is "keep on paper". |

## Watch / avoid

* Rain, snow and cold-only plays: watch only (rain faded; snow n = 47).
* The weather-only model lean: not used for CFB (walk-forward 52.8% at P ≥ 53%).
* Indoor venues and neutral sites without venue coordinates.

Lines: The Odds API (Pinnacle, else DraftKings) when `ODDS_API_KEY` is set;
otherwise ESPN's DraftKings feed when it responds. With neither, triggers arrive
as WATCH / no price.
