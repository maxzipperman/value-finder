# Thesis research: weather and NFL passing

A review of Max Zipperman's 2014 Claremont McKenna senior thesis, *Quantifying the Impact of Temperature and Wind on NFL Passing and Rushing Performance*. It covers similar public and academic work before and since the thesis, which findings have held up, and referee-style feedback on the paper.
