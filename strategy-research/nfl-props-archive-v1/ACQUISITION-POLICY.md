# Comprehensive archive preparation — proposed policy, not paid authority

## Scope and finite stages

All documented NFL and CFB player-prop keys, including alternates and touchdown/defensive
markets, are the starting catalog. Historical keys absent from today's catalog
remain acquisition evidence and require a dated catalog expansion before purchase;
never silently discard them. NFL and CFB 2023–2025, including preseason, regular season,
playoffs and unmatched/provider-only listings, is the first unsealed acquisition
universe. May 3, 2023 at 05:30 UTC is the archive start. The unsealed preparation
cutoff is February 10, 2026 at 00:00 UTC. The ongoing 2026 season is a later, sealed
acquisition stage requiring distinct storage and role review.

First tranche: T−24h and the existing scheduled-safe close proxy T−10min. Six-time
grid: add T−72h, T−48h, T−6h and T−1h. All offsets floor to the five-minute grid.
Actual first-play evidence may only exclude/certify, never admit a late quote.
An acquisition close proxy does not replace the registered grader's eligibility
or imply an executable quote. Preserve every unbound/ambiguous opportunity.

1. Reuse authenticated, completed cached event/listing metadata. Propose a finite
   daily listing sweep at 06:00 UTC from the archive start through the cutoff,
   subtracting exact compatible historical-events caches. This discovers preseason
   and missing event IDs. A daily sweep can miss transient listings; compare it
   with schedule metadata, keep unresolved coverage gaps, and do not claim every
   possible five-minute listing was observed. No paid call occurs in preparation.
2. For every decision slot, historical event-markets discovery covers the proposed
   core regions: us, us2, eu (domestic books plus sharp benchmarks). Deduplicate books
   across regions. UK/AU/CA/FR/SE/FI, DFS and exchanges are optional cost lines,
   not a prerequisite to domestic data. Record historical availability at that slot rather than assuming
   every current book existed in 2023. Preserve provider-only IDs and conflicting
   clocks; book/market additions change the exact list and require fresh review.
3. Build explicit book/market price requests from the discovered cells. Subtract
   completed compatible prior **requested coverage**, including recorded absence;
   distinguish absent quotes from returned quotes. Partition residual markets by
   identical residual book sets into batches of at most ten books. This avoids
   repaying cached subsets when an expanded request would have a different key.
   The partition is deterministic, not a claim of global cost optimality.
4. Freeze T24/close first. Freeze the four earlier-time additions separately after
   their exact availability/reuse costs are known. Any denser grid is another
   increment, not permission to consume the remainder of the month.

The existing ten-book panel is a cost benchmark and reuse comparator, **not** a
substitute for historically available domestic/sharp book scope; worldwide scope is separately costed. Complete core-book price cost cannot be exact until discovery
supplies each slot's market/book cells. The first metadata list is finite now;
unknown future event IDs are never invented to make a price packet look complete.

## Compatibility and conservative stops

Reuse requires exact provider event, requested timestamp, source/URL, response
format and book/market coverage, authenticated completed purchase/cache evidence,
unchanged response bytes, HTTP 200, matching event/sport and snapshot no later than
decision time with lag at most 600 seconds. Preserve requested-but-absent cells as
an availability observation, not a priced row. Live captures and provider metadata
with different request times do not substitute for historical decision snapshots.
Conflicting copies or unreadable receipts block overlapping work.

Pending, timed-out, halted-uncertain or otherwise unverified prior attempts block
every overlapping book/market cell, including supersets, differently ordered
parameters and replacement endpoints. Full reservations remain. Never resend,
repair or release them automatically. A changed content/list/cap or out-of-policy
state requires separately reviewed exact authority.

## Executor acceptance requirements

This preparation module has no transport and no spending authority. A new executor
must receive independent technical review and hub current-head agreement. Under
the existing shared global lock it must authenticate all source/list/eligibility/
policy/dependency bytes, execute verified captured bytes, revalidate every prior
receipt and all shared registrations/ledgers, bind the account reconciliation and
finite missing policy to live active hub approval, and exhaust completed authority.
Verify live revocation before every GET; zero retries/redirects. Persist reservation
before send and receipt/cache before completion. A pending crash halts; a clean
allowed restart sends only untouched IDs. Never lower reservations to actual bills.

The first 250,000-credit tranche is not automatically expanded. New list/cap must
fit the owner's prepaid monthly scope, remaining balance less the reserve floor,
all preserved shared debits and cumulative ceilings. Hub approves the exact new
bounded stage. Book/market availability discovery costs one credit per request;
price reservation is ten times requested market count times ceil(book count/10).
Actual billing may be lower; that does not release conservative reservations.

Before moving stages, produce outcome-blind per-year/phase/market/book/slot coverage,
unmatched/ambiguous counts and every missing/error. The comprehensive price packet
is NOT READY while universe, availability or executor verification is incomplete.
